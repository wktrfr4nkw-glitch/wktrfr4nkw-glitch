### a11y-fixer
**Shopify accessibility audit & remediation platform**

A deterministic accessibility auditing system designed for small agencies and independent Shopify merchants.

Rather than acting as a thin wrapper around existing scanners, the platform combines established accessibility tooling with custom checks, evidence normalization, scoring, scan-integrity controls and remediation workflows.

Findings can be translated into structured developer or AI-agent handoffs, with selected low-risk fixes automated directly.

`TypeScript` · `Python` · `Shopify` · `PostgreSQL` · `Background Jobs`
